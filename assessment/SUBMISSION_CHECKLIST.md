# Submission checklist

- [ ] Part A interview completed with a real manufacturing/processing/repair business for at least 20 minutes.
- [ ] Part A one-page notes, two exact quotes and actual workflow documented.
- [ ] Hand-drawn process map photographed.
- [ ] At least two tracking-method photos taken with permission and personal details blurred.
- [ ] Owner contact details shared only in submission email, with permission.
- [ ] Part B requirement document within four pages, based on the provided client pack.
- [ ] Evidence and assumptions distinguished; no unverified facts presented as confirmed.
- [ ] Part C handoff note within half a page; no pricing.
- [ ] Part D LinkedIn post/ad anonymised and based on Part A.
- [ ] Part E five-minute camera-on video recorded.
- [ ] AI note of 5–10 lines accurately reflects actual use.
- [ ] PDF/Google Doc link, video link, AI note and permitted business contact details sent by replying to the assignment email within the official 48-hour window.
